# TechNova Solutions Knowledge Base

## About TechNova Solutions
TechNova Solutions is a small IT services company based in Surat, Gujarat. We build websites, mobile apps, custom business software, and AI automation solutions for small and medium businesses, and we offer ongoing maintenance and support. Our team includes developers, designers, a business analyst, and a support team. We work with clients across India.

## Services Overview
TechNova offers five services:
1. Website Development
2. Mobile App Development
3. Custom Software / ERP / CRM Setup
4. AI and Automation Solutions
5. Maintenance and Support

All prices below are approximate ranges. The final price is decided only after a discovery call and a written proposal from our team.

## Website Development
- Price range: ₹30,000 to ₹1,50,000
- Typical timeline: 2 to 6 weeks
- Includes: business websites, landing pages, e-commerce stores, and portfolio sites. All sites are mobile-friendly and SEO-ready.
- Price depends on the number of pages, design complexity, and features such as online payments or admin panels.

## Mobile App Development
- Price range: ₹2,00,000 to ₹8,00,000
- Typical timeline: 2 to 4 months
- Includes: Android and iOS apps (single codebase using Flutter or React Native), UI/UX design, backend and API development, and publishing to the app stores.
- Examples: delivery apps, booking apps, e-learning apps, and customer service apps.
- Price depends on the number of features, platforms, and third-party integrations.

## Custom Software / ERP / CRM Setup
- Price range: ₹3,00,000 to ₹12,00,000
- Typical timeline: 3 to 6 months
- Includes: custom business software, ERP and CRM setup, Odoo customization, dashboards, and reporting tools.
- Suitable for businesses that want to manage inventory, sales, HR, accounting, or customers in one system.
- Price depends on the number of modules, users, and integrations with existing systems.

## AI and Automation Solutions
- Price range: ₹1,00,000 to ₹6,00,000
- Typical timeline: 1 to 3 months
- Includes: AI chatbots, workflow automation (for example n8n), document processing, lead qualification bots, and integrations with tools like Slack, Google Sheets, and CRMs.
- Examples: a support chatbot that answers customer FAQs, or an automation that sends daily reports to a team.
- Price depends on the number of workflows, integrations, and AI features.

## Maintenance and Support
- Price: monthly plans starting from ₹10,000 per month
- Includes: bug fixes, security updates, performance monitoring, small feature changes, and priority support.
- Plans:
  - Basic: ₹10,000 per month. Bug fixes and updates, with email support.
  - Standard: ₹20,000 per month. Everything in Basic, plus small feature changes and faster response.
  - Premium: ₹35,000 per month. Everything in Standard, plus priority support and monthly performance reports.
- Maintenance can be added to any project built by TechNova or to an existing system built by someone else, after a quick review.

## Our Process
1. Enquiry: the client contacts us through the website or chat.
2. Discovery call: our team discusses requirements in detail, usually 30 to 45 minutes.
3. Proposal and quote: we send a document with scope, timeline, and final price within 3 to 5 working days.
4. Agreement and advance payment: the contract is signed and the project starts after an advance.
5. Development: work is done in stages with regular progress updates.
6. Testing and delivery: we test everything and hand over the final product.
7. Support: after delivery, there is a free support period, and optional maintenance plans after that.

## Engagement Models
- Fixed price: a set price for a clearly defined scope. Best for websites and small apps.
- Dedicated team / monthly: a developer or team works for you on a monthly basis. Best for long or changing projects.
